How to use any of this: a blue chip (CC0, OFL, MIT, ISC) means client work, paid products and resale, no thinking required. Plain chips are free but limited - read the card, because the limits are not cosmetic. Which matters most: Fontshare forbids subsetting and format conversion, so you cannot make web fonts from it; Shapefest's own terms contradict each other on commercial use; 3Dicons is only CC0 for the original set. Boards and screenshots carry no licence at all - rebuild the idea and publish your own version.
